# Bid checklist fixture (2026-10-18)

A bid passes only if every line is yes. Run it before the packet goes to review.

- Lines 1 and 2 quote the client's own words and name one specific risk. No greeting, no credentials.
- One proof item, sourced to the canon or a live Upwork page, with a link.
- One bounded next step. No free plan, no free discovery.
- One closing question that needs the post to have been read.
- 100 to 180 words, no dashes, no staged contrast, humanizer pass done.
- Screening answers: first sentence answers, under 40 words each.
- No email, Calendly or other off-platform contact.
- Engineer named or promised before commitment; margin at or above $8 an hour.
- Job fresh (hours), proposals under 15, payment verified, passed scam, chance and fit.
- Logged with its variant in the ledger.

Bad: "Hi, thanks for posting! I am an expert Next.js developer with 15 years..."
Good: opens with the client's phrase and the risk they did not name.
