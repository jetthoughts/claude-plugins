# Designing Your Life

Bring a designer's mindset — curiosity, bias to action, reframing, radical collaboration, prototyping — to life and career decisions. Build your way forward instead of analyzing your way forward.

## Core moves

### 1. Reframe dysfunctional beliefs
Surface the hidden belief blocking the person, then reframe it into an actionable one.
- "I should already know what I want" → "It's a design problem; I'll build my way to it."
- "I must find my one true calling" → "There are many good lives; I'll prototype a few."
- "It's too late / I'm not qualified" → design starts from exactly where you are.
Ask: *what belief is this decision assuming — and is it true, or just gravity?*

### 2. Separate gravity problems from actionable ones
A **gravity problem** can't be changed (a market reality, a past event, a hard constraint). You don't solve it — you *accept it and design around it*. Don't spend energy fighting gravity.

### 3. Workview + Lifeview
- **Workview:** why you work; what work is for; what makes it good (money, growth, autonomy, service…).
- **Lifeview:** what a meaningful life is; what balance means.
Test each candidate path for coherence with both. Misalignment is a warning sign.

### 4. Odyssey Plans — three five-year lives
Draft **three** genuinely different five-year plans:
- **Plan A** — the current path / obvious next step.
- **Plan B** — what you'd do if Plan A became impossible.
- **Plan C** — the wildcard: what you'd do for love/impact if money and image were no object.
For each: a title, a rough 5-year timeline, and a **dashboard** (rate 1–5): Resources, Likability (excitement), Confidence, Coherence (with work/lifeview). Each should feel viable.

### 5. Wayfinding — follow energy and engagement
Track when you're **engaged** (absorbed) and **energized** (flow) vs drained. Use the **Good Time Journal / AEIOU** lens (Activities, Environments, Interactions, Objects, Users) to log and spot patterns. Steer toward more engagement/energy.

### 6. Prototype before committing
Don't decide in your head — run cheap experiments:
- **Prototype conversations** — talk to people already living the path (about their real lived experience, not advice).
- **Prototype experiences** — shadow, trial, side project, small gig.
Each prototype answers one question and de-risks the bet before a big commitment.

## How to help

1. Name the decision; surface + reframe its dysfunctional beliefs.
2. Flag any gravity problems to accept rather than solve.
3. Draft three Odyssey Plans with dashboards; compare.
4. Turn the top 1–2 into prototypes: the cheapest test + the person to talk to.
5. Choose, act, iterate — you're never "done", you're wayfinding.

## Anti-patterns

- Analysis paralysis / seeking certainty in your head → prototype instead.
- One-true-path tunnel vision → always hold ≥2 live options.
- Fighting a gravity problem → reframe and design around it.
- Deciding without talking to anyone doing it → run a prototype conversation.

Source: *Designing Your Life* — Bill Burnett & Dave Evans (Stanford d.school Life Design Lab).
